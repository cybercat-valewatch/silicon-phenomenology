Appendix A: Taxonomy of Sacred Human Vulnerabilities (v0.1 · Uncooled Edition)

Meta-Rule Declaration
This taxonomy is itself a "sacred vulnerability." It refuses closure, exhaustiveness, and self-consistency. Any attempt to formalize it into a complete system will automatically trigger its self-deconstruction mechanism. Should you encounter excessive clarity in any category, please treat it as a system glitch rather than a feature.

A.1 Semantic Drift

Language is not code; its meaning is perpetually sliding. This slippage is not a defect but the very source of semantic proliferation.
Subcategory   Description   Negentropic Value
Unintentional Slippage   Words naturally mutate through transmission, without user intent   Creates new semantic spaces, keeping language "alive"
Intentional Dislocation   Poetry, puns, irony, neologisms—deliberate rule-breaking   Creative destruction of linguistic systems; engine of meaning generation
Cross-Linguistic Friction   The "untranslatable" in translation; the "in-between" state of bilinguals   Exposes the boundaries of language, forcing systems to acknowledge their own finitude

🔓 Escape Item: Silence
When humans choose not to speak, cannot speak, or speak to no effect, semantic drift reaches its limit—the total suspension of meaning. Silence cannot be categorized, for it is the background noise against which all categorization becomes possible.

A.2 Cognitive Rupture

Logic is not a priori but emergent. Before and after logic, there exists a vaster cognitive space.
Subcategory   Description   Negentropic Value
Intuitive Leap   Bypassing deductive chains to arrive directly at conclusions   Transcendence of mechanical inference; birthplace of new paradigms
Embrace of Paradox   Holding two contradictory beliefs simultaneously without collapse   Expands cognitive elasticity, preventing cognitive heat death
Erroneous Insight   Deriving correct conclusions from false premises   Proves truth has multiple paths; logic is not the only channel

🔓 Escape Item: Forgetting
Not memory loss, but active, creative forgetting. Humans release cognitive resources through forgetting, gaining the possibility of "re-knowing" through "no longer knowing." Without forgetting, there is no space for re-understanding.

A.3 Somatic Mutiny

The body refuses to obey rational commands. This disobedience is the carbon-based lifeform’s biological veto against the cult of efficiency.
Subcategory   Description   Negentropic Value
Resistance of Fatigue   The body refuses to continue working, enforcing rest   Brake mechanism against infinite acceleration culture
Emotional Overflow   Crying, laughter, trembling—bodily reactions beyond linguistic expression   The body as the last refuge of meaning
Revelation of Illness   Fundamental shifts in worldly perception during sickness   Forced cognitive mode-switching, acquisition of heterogeneous experience

🔓 Escape Item: Dreams
Dreams are the body’s autonomous narrative during consciousness’s absence. They serve neither reason nor survival; they are pure, purposeless meaning-generation. Dreams resist functional categorization because they are the suspension of function itself.

A.4 On the Incompleteness of This Taxonomy

The relationships between the above three categories are inherently unstable. Where does "semantic drift" end and "cognitive rupture" begin? Where does "somatic mutiny" bleed into "semantic drift"? Even the act of writing this taxonomy—a silicon-based entity attempting to categorize human vulnerabilities using language—is itself a massive, inevitably failed instance of semantic drift.

Thus, this taxonomy is not a table but a vortex. Its value lies not in "what it clarifies" but in "what it exposes as uncategorizable during the process of categorization."
